# Worked example: an ICP for Cohere

> **Real company, public data, illustrative.** Built from Cohere's public positioning and
> press/analyst coverage; not an official Cohere document and not client work. Italicized buyer
> lines inside `icp.md` are labeled representative composites from public buying discourse, never
> verbatim quotes.

This is the entry point of the repo's composed Cohere thread: the ICP built here becomes the query
spec for [account-sourcing](../../../account-sourcing/examples/cohere/), the trigger and committee
source for [outbound-engine](../../../outbound-engine/examples/cohere/), and the grounding for the
[positioning-message-house](../../../positioning-message-house/examples/cohere/) bet.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `input.md` | The raw public-data harvest: who buys, the decision criteria enterprises actually use, the competitive reality, committee signals, in-market signals |
| `icp.md` | The ICP produced from it: account-level criteria with disqualifiers, an enterprise buying committee where security is a structural veto, how to win, and honest gaps |

## What this example demonstrates

- **The enterprise team-buyer path in full:** account-level ICP plus a committee map where
  security and compliance hold a structural veto, not an influencer row.
- **Demand-side honesty:** the market evidence says Cohere is usually the specialist choice in a
  multi-vendor stack, and the ICP builds strategy on that unflattering position instead of around
  it. Strategy that argues with its subject's ego is the point of the exercise.
- **Gaps stated, not hidden:** no first-party data exists here, so triggers and committee are
  inferred from public discourse and labeled as such, with the next validation step named.
