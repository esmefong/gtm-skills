# North, deployed in more places your data already lives

North now runs on sovereign infrastructure in additional regions across Canada and Europe. Same
platform, same guarantee: your data stays inside your environment, and Cohere never sees it.

The expansion covers on-premise, VPC, and air-gapped deployment on partner infrastructure.
Enterprises in regulated sectors can build retrieval and agents on their own knowledge, in their
own languages, behind their own security review.

The review is the point. Private deployment exists so your compliance team can say yes with
evidence: reference architectures to hold us to, data commitments in writing, infrastructure that
keeps everything in-country.

Your data. Your infrastructure. More places to run both.
